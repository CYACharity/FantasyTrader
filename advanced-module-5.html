Value at Risk (VaR): Three Approaches

VaR answers: "What's my worst expected loss at 95% confidence over 1 day?" If 1-day VaR is $100K, you expect losses exceeding $100K on only 1 in 20 days. Three methods calculate VaR: parametric (assumes normal distribution), historical (uses past data), and Monte Carlo (simulates outcomes).

Critical Limitation

VaR ignores tail risk. It says "my loss won't exceed $100K 95% of the time" but tells you nothing about catastrophic 1-in-100-days losses that can be -$1M. This is why banks also use expected shortfall (CVaR).

Stress Testing & Scenario Analysis

Stress testing applies extreme market scenarios: -20% stocks, Fed +5% rates, VIX +200%. Scenario analysis asks "What if 2008 crashes again?" Test your portfolio against 2008, 2020 COVID crash, and 2022 rate spike. This reveals concentrated risks VaR misses.

Options Greeks: Measuring Sensitivity

Delta = change in option value per $1 move in stock. Call delta ranges 0-1; put delta ranges -1 to 0. Gamma = change in delta. High gamma near expiration = large delta swings. Theta = daily time decay. Vega = change per 1% volatility move. Short positions lose on vega spikes.

Greek Measures Effect on Long Call Effect on Short Call
Delta (Δ) Stock price change Positive: +$0.50 per stock $1 move Negative: loses on up move
Gamma (Γ) Delta acceleration Positive: higher moves=more gain Negative: losses accelerate
Theta (Θ) Time decay Negative: loses daily value Positive: gains from decay
Vega (ν) Volatility change Positive: vol spike helps Negative: vol spike hurts

Portfolio Insurance: Hedging Catastrophic Risk

Portfolio insurance combines long stocks with put options to cap losses. If you own $1M portfolio and buy $100K protection via puts (5% OTM), your max loss is the put cost (premium). Expensive but essential for risk-averse institutional investors. Dynamic hedging rebalances daily to maintain protection.

Key Takeaways

  1. VaR quantifies expected losses at a confidence level but ignores tail risk beyond the percentile.
  2. Stress testing reveals portfolio concentration and extreme scenario vulnerabilities VaR misses.
  3. Delta measures directional exposure; Gamma measures acceleration risk; Theta measures decay; Vega measures vol sensitivity.
  4. Portfolio insurance caps maximum loss using puts, essential for institutional risk management.
  5. Risk management philosophy: quantify what you can, stress test what you can't, always hedge tail risk.
