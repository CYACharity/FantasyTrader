Joining a League

League Size4 – 12 players
How to Join6-character join code from the host
League LockNo new players once the draft starts

The Snake Draft

Every league drafts live, together. The pick order reverses each round — if you pick last in round one, you pick first in round two. Every player drafts 14 stocks, and 11 are in play each week.

One owner per stock. Once a ticker is drafted, it's off the board for everyone else. All listed stocks are available — no restricted universe.

Capital Allocation

Starting CapitalSet by the host (default $100,000)
Deploying ItChoose share counts for each drafted stock
Undeployed CashSits idle — it never grows
OverspendingImpossible — the slider caps at your cash

Scoring & Standings

Standings rank players by total portfolio return — the live market value of your shares against what you paid. Prices track the real market during trading hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET).

Trading Restrictions

Short SellingNot allowed
Day TradingNot allowed
Your EdgeWhat you draft and how you weight it

Winning

The player with the highest portfolio return when the season ends takes the crown. Ties break by total portfolio value. Talk is encouraged; whining is not.
