Alternative Investments

Diversify beyond stocks and bonds with REITs, commodities, crypto, and private equity strategies.

8 min read
Sources: NAREIT, CME, CFA Institute
Last updated Mar 2026

Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)

REITs own and operate income-producing real estate. Unlike owning a property directly, REITs are liquid, diversified, and tax-advantaged. Equity REITs own buildings; mortgage REITs hold loans. REITs must distribute 90% of taxable income as dividends. Average REIT yield is 3-5%, beating bonds.

Key Point

REITs are interest-rate sensitive. When rates rise, REIT valuations fall because income is discounted more heavily. Diversification benefit comes from low correlation with stocks during equity downturns.

Commodities & Commodity Futures

Commodities include precious metals (gold, silver), energy (oil, natural gas), and agriculture (wheat, corn). Direct ownership requires storage. Commodity futures provide leveraged exposure. Oil futures (WTI) and gold futures (GC) are most liquid. Commodities hedge inflation and geopolitical risk.

Why Own Commodities?

Negative correlation with stocks in inflation regimes. Oil prices spike during geopolitical crises. Gold maintains value during currency collapse. Diversification benefit during equity selloffs.

Cryptocurrency & Blockchain Basics

Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH) are decentralized digital currencies secured by blockchain. Bitcoin's supply is capped at 21 million, creating scarcity. Ethereum enables smart contracts. Crypto markets operate 24/7 with high volatility (50-100% annual swings). Institutional adoption has increased through spot ETFs (since Jan 2024).

Private Equity & Venture Capital Overview

Private equity firms buy companies, improve operations, and exit for profit. Venture capital funds early-stage startups. These illiquid investments require 5-10 year lockups but historically return 15-25% IRR. Accredited investors minimum $200K income or $1M net worth required.

Key Takeaways

  1. REITs provide real estate exposure with monthly/quarterly dividends and interest-rate sensitivity.
  2. Commodities offer inflation protection and negative correlation to equities during crises.
  3. Cryptocurrency is high-volatility, 24/7, and increasingly institutional-grade with spot ETF access.
  4. Private equity delivers 15-25% returns but requires 5-10 year lockups and accredited investor status.
  5. A diversified portfolio includes 5-15% alternatives to reduce overall volatility.
